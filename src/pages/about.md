So — who am I?

I’m _Devadathan_ (or _Dev_, usually because that’s easier), a 2024 Computer Science Engineering
graduate, currently working as an engineer at [Sage](https://fylehq.com) (formerly Fyle).

I mostly work on backend systems — APIs, migrations, background jobs, and long-lived parts of
systems that need to stay understandable over time. I tend to gravitate toward work where small
decisions compound, and where clarity matters more than cleverness.

Outside of work, I spend a lot of time thinking — sometimes about systems and technology, sometimes
about people, patterns, and why certain things feel harder or simpler than they should. I enjoy
quiet experiments, reading, wandering through ideas without needing to conclude them, and building
small things just to see how they behave.

This site is where I write things down so they don’t disappear into my head forever. Some of it is
polished, some of it is just me thinking out loud. Both are intentional.
