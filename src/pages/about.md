So — who am I?

I’m _Devadathan_ (or _Dev_, usually because that’s easier), a 2024 Computer Science Engineering
graduate, currently working as an engineer at [Sage (formerly Fyle)](https://fylehq.com).

I mostly work on backend systems — APIs, migrations, background jobs, and the quieter parts of
software that tend to live longer than their original context. I care deeply about the craft of
engineering: how systems are shaped, how decisions age, and how small choices can make software
either brittle or calm to work with over time.

That care spills into how I work day-to-day. I keep a well-worn set of tools, configurations, and
notes — things like my dotfiles, workflows, and small utilities — not out of obsession, but because
they help me think clearly and move deliberately. I value simplicity, ownership, and software that
respects both the people who use it and the people who maintain it.

Outside of work, I spend a lot of time thinking — sometimes about technology, sometimes about
people, patterns, and ideas that don’t demand immediate conclusions. I admire open and libre
software, quiet experimentation, and communities that build in the open without trying to sell
everything they touch.

This is the place where I write things down so they don’t disappear into my head forever. Some of it is
polished, some of it is just me thinking out loud. Both are intentional.
